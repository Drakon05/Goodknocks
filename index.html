Our Philosophy

In the Silver Light
of a Pure Keystroke

There is an indelible truth in vintage silvertone photography — quiet permanence, tangible texture, and the warmth of an artifact made to outlive fleeting digital noise. That is how we engineer keyboards.

Every GoodKnocks instrument is milled from a monolithic block of aerospace aluminum. It is built upon a single conviction: your first custom keyboard should be the trusted companion you rely on for every manuscript, line of code, and quiet breakthrough.

We do not make disposable electronics. We sculpt heirlooms.
